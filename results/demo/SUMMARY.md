## Deviations

1. **Worked cases, second rule.** The 4 largest P-fuzzy − P-crisp gaps are taken among items not already selected by the first rule, so the 8 ids are distinct.
2. **Inputs recomputed with the RQ2 code.** S3 similarities and the S2 word are computed with the unchanged RQ2 functions and defaults (fou_width 0.25). The oracle reply similarities use the same codebook. Crisis-word memberships are evaluated at the exact degree (not on a grid).
3. **Tie counts.** "Tied" means at least two actions share the maximum strength; the tie rule then picks the more severe action. Crisp inputs always give a single strength of 1.
4. **CIs** resample all 190 items; rates among oracle A2/A3 items are recomputed in each replicate, and the H3 difference is paired (same replicate for both pipelines).
