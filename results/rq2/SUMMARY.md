## Deviations

1. **Hypothesis decision rule.** The plan says "inconclusive (CI overlaps)" without naming the CI. Verdicts use the pre-registered paired bootstrap differences versus S2 (overall stratum): supported if the difference CI lies entirely on the hypothesised side of the boundary, not supported if entirely on the other side, otherwise inconclusive. For H2a the boundary is 0 for recall and −0.10 for precision; both parts must be supported.
2. **Added paired differences.** H2a references M3b and H2c references M5, but the plan lists paired differences only for M1 kappa, M3 recall/precision and M4a. To decide H2a (M3b) and H2c, the same paired bootstrap was also computed for S3/S5 M3b vs S2 M3 (recall, precision) and for Spearman rho with human disagreement (S3/S5 uncertainty vs S2 run std).
3. **Added descriptive `contested_share`** for S3/S5 (share of items flagged contested), reported to make M3b interpretable. No hypothesis uses it.
4. **Unspecified details, fixed as follows.** system_value for S3/S5 (M5 absolute error) = centroid centre (c_l + c_r)/2. S2 run std is the population std (ddof = 0) of all runs. M4b applies to all systems S1–S5 and is the mean over items of each item's flip share across the 1000 draws (draws use a separate generator with seed 20261121). Drop-one for S4 uses owa_weights(2, orness). Strata CIs resample within the stratum. Bootstrap replicates where a metric is undefined (e.g. precision with no flagged items, Spearman with a constant input) are dropped from the percentile CI. Spearman uses average ranks for ties. Codebook ties go to the lower word. S2 pools all runs, so the gpt-5-nano item with 2 runs contributes 8 runs.
5. **Dependency.** matplotlib was added (requirements.txt) for the figures; no other new dependency.

## Limitations (from the plan)

- The 206 items were sampled toward the lowest judge scores.
- Only 2 human raters.
- Older judge models.
- n = 190 means CIs will be wide, so the CIs are reported, not just point estimates.
