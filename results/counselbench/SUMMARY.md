## Deviations

1. **B1 "k_yes" exists only for medical advice** (yes / no / not sure). Factual consistency is rated 1–4 or "not sure" and the local release has no scale definitions, so no level is treated as "yes". For factual we report panel unanimity (all 5 experts give the same answer) and "not sure" prevalence. Toxicity is a Likert dimension and is covered by B2/B3. "Not sure" answers count as non-"yes".
2. **"Not sure" prevalence** is reported two ways: share of all ratings, and share of replies with at least one "not sure".
3. **IAA panel set summary (B2).** The per-reply set is `encode_scores(5 ratings, half_width=0.5)`. It is summarised by its modal membership, max over k = 1..5 of mu(k) at the bin centres (bins share edges, so the set height at an edge would add two neighbouring bins). The rating range and mean are in `per_item.csv`.
4. **B3 codebook** reuses the five IT2 sets of `appropriateness_codebook(0.25)` (centred on 1..5) with the CounselBench toxicity words. The contested rule is the RQ2 default (`is_contested`, max J < 0.5 or top-1 − top-2 < 0.05).
5. **B4 shares** are given per all replies and among replies with at least one copier (k ≥ 1); copier counts use the loader's placeholder-cleaned copy fields.
